About Us

We are KaalYatri.

A small team of scientists, history teachers, and travel lovers. We believe time travel is the next great way to see the world.

Our story

For a long time, people dreamed of visiting other planets and stars. We thought the next big thing in travel would be space. We were wrong. The next big thing is travel through time.

Our time machines are safe, fully insured, and led by some of India's best historians and scientists. You don't change history — you just see it, hear it, taste it, and come home with stories you will never forget.
