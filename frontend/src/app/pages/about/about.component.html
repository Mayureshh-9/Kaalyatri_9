About Us

We are KaalYatri.

A small team of travel lovers. We believe personalized travel is the next great way to see the world.

Our story

We are five friends who came together to start KaalYatri — a startup with a crazy dream. What began as late-night conversations about history, travel, and technology slowly turned into reality.

Aniket Kadam, our globetrotting cinematographer, has traveled across India and beyond, capturing stories and moments through his lens. His passion for storytelling and immersive experiences became the heart of KaalYatri.

Mrunmay Jadhav and Siddhant Sonawane are our dynamic duo — the cool guys with unmatched energy and the ability to sell and achieve anything they set their minds to. Their drive keeps pushing the vision forward.

Chirag and Mayuresh, the IT nerds of the group, handle all the tech wizardry — from building systems to making sure everything runs smoothly behind the scenes.

Together, we created KaalYatri — where time travel isn't just science fiction anymore. Our time machines are safe, fully insured, and designed to let you experience history like never before.
